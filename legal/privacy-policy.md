## 1. Who we are

Verdancy is a plant identification and plant-care app. You take or select a photo of a plant, we help identify it and diagnose problems, and we generate care guidance. Subscribers get additional features such as tailored care plans, a light meter, and the Plant Buddy companion.

If you have questions about this policy, contact us at **[SUPPORT EMAIL]**.

## 2. Information we collect

### Information you provide

- **Account information.** When you sign in with Sign in with Apple, Google, or email, we receive and store an account identifier and, depending on your choices, your email address and name. If you use Sign in with Apple's "Hide My Email," we receive only the private relay address Apple provides.
- **Plant photos.** When you identify or diagnose a plant, you provide a photo from your camera or photo library.
- **Plant and garden data.** Names, notes, care schedules, "done" actions, and other data you create about your plants.

### Information collected automatically

- **Subscription status.** We work with RevenueCat and the Apple App Store to manage subscriptions. We receive your subscription and entitlement status and a subscription identifier. We do **not** receive or store your full payment card details — payment is handled by Apple.
- **Basic technical data.** Limited operational data needed to run the service (for example, request logs and error information). We do not use advertising identifiers (IDFA) and do not track you across other apps or websites.

### Information we do **not** collect

- We do **not** collect your precise location.
- We do **not** use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs.
- We do **not** sell your personal information.

## 3. How we use your information

We use the information above to:

- Identify plants and diagnose plant-health problems from your photos.
- Generate care plans, reminders, and other care guidance.
- Create and maintain your account and authenticate you.
- Manage your subscription and unlock subscriber features.
- Operate, secure, debug, and improve the App.
- Communicate with you about the service (for example, support responses).

---

## 4. How your photos are handled

Your privacy around photos is central to how Verdancy is built:

- **Identify and diagnose photos** you submit are sent to our AI provider for analysis and are **not stored on our servers** — the image bytes are processed to return a result and then discarded.
- **Photos you choose to save** to a plant in your garden are stored in a **private, per-user cloud storage bucket**. They are accessible only to your account and are never made public or shared with other users.
- Photos are uploaded and downloaded using short-lived, per-request secure links tied to your account. One user can never access another user's photos.

---

## 5. AI processing and third parties

To identify plants, diagnose issues, generate care plans, and create the Plant Buddy artwork, we send the relevant photo and/or text to **Google's Gemini AI service**. That data is used to produce your result. We do not send your name, email, or account contact details to the AI provider along with the image.

The Plant Buddy companion art is generated **per plant species** (not per user) and is shared across all users who have that species — no personal information is embedded in it.
